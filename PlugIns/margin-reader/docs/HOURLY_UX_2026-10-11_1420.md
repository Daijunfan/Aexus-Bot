## MarginNote 4 user-view comparison

Official MN4 offers a contextual hand-tool menu and 16 customizable excerpt colors. Margin Reader already exposes 16 choices, but selection-to-color keyboard operation, name visibility and small-screen tap targets were weaker. This iteration focused on the existing excerpt palette rather than introducing new product features. Manual: https://manual.marginnote.com.cn/mn4/hand-tool-pop-up-menu-bar/ and https://manual.marginnote.com.cn/mn4/customize-card-excerpt-colors/.
